## LinkedIn

A $3,000 HSA contribution can cost you as little as $2,340 out of pocket if you're in the 22% tax bracket. That's math an emergency fund can't replicate, and it's why a cash cushion alone isn't a real defense against medical debt.

For optometry and medical practice owners, this matters. An HSA lets contributions go in tax-free, grow tax-free, and come out tax-free for qualified expenses. No other account in the tax code does all three. Add the 7.5% AGI medical deduction threshold and smart FSA timing, and you've got a three-layer strategy that actually lowers what a health crisis costs, not just a fund that buys you time.

Jared Hammack, CPA, breaks down the contribution limits and year-end timing moves worth making before December 31st. Read the full breakdown: https://rootadvisors.com/resources/why-tax-advantaged-planning-is-the-real-defense-against-medi
