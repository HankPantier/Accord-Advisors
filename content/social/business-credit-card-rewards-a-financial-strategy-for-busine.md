## LinkedIn

A $95 annual fee card needs roughly $4,750 in spend on a 2% cash-back rate before it earns you anything. That's the math most business owners skip when they pick a card for the sign-up bonus instead of their actual spending pattern.

For optometry practices, dental offices, and service businesses spending $3,000 to $60,000 a month on supplies, lab fees, and subscriptions, the card's structure matters more than the flashy offer. An established practice with $500,000 or more in annual card spend can realistically pull $5,000 to $10,000 a year in value from a category-bonus card matched to its biggest expense line.

Jared Hammack, CPA, breaks down the tax treatment of rewards and how to reconcile redemptions cleanly in the books, so a rewards program strengthens the numbers instead of complicating them.
