## LinkedIn

A Bloomington optometrist missed a lease renewal deadline and it cost her practice an extra $14,000 a year in rent. She wasn't careless. She was exhausted after months of closing the office at 9 p.m. to reconcile insurance claims herself.

That's the real cost of burnout. Exhausted decisions show up on the balance sheet as missed deadlines, bad hires, and unread contracts. Jared Hammack, CPA, has watched this pattern for nearly 40 years across optometry, dental, and service businesses in Bloomington and Indianapolis. Owners who skip outsourced accounting aren't saving money. They're trading a fixed monthly fee for far more expensive mistakes.

Rest isn't a reward you earn after success. It's part of what protects your judgment and your bottom line.
