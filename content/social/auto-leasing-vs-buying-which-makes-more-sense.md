## LinkedIn

A $45,000 SUV can lease for $550 a month or cost $780 a month on a 60-month loan. The lease looks cheaper until you count what you actually own at the end: often $15,000 to $20,000 in resale value after five years of buying.

For a dentist hauling equipment between two Bloomington offices, buying a used truck at 80% business use can trigger a Section 179 deduction that meaningfully offsets that year's taxable income. Leasing only lets you deduct the business-use portion of the payment, spread evenly, with no lump sum.

Optometrists, dentists, and service business owners weighing a practice vehicle need the real math, not a dealership's monthly payment pitch. Jared Hammack, CPA, walks through this calculation with medical and optometry clients every year.
