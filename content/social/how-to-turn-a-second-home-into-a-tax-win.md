## LinkedIn

Rent your Lake Monroe cabin for one weekend and collect $2,800? That money can be completely tax-free.

The IRS 14-day rule says if you rent a second home for 14 days or fewer in a year, you don't report the rental income at all, no matter what you charge. Cross into 15+ rental days, and your personal-use days start deciding whether you get full depreciation and expense deductions or a capped rental loss carried forward to future years.

For optometrists, physicians, and business owners in Bloomington weighing a second home against reinvesting in their practice, that distinction changes the math before a purchase agreement gets signed. Jared Hammack, CPA, walks through where the lines fall and how to document rental days and personal use so the deduction holds up under audit.
