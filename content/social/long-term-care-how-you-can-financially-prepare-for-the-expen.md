## LinkedIn

A private nursing home room in Indiana now runs close to $100,000 a year, and Medicare covers none of it. Assisted living isn't much better at $55,000 to $65,000, and in-home aides can top $60,000 for full-time care. Most families plan for a few months of rehab after surgery. The real risk is five or more years of care, and the bill doesn't stop when savings run out.

The tools that work, long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance benefits, and dedicated self-funding, only work if you set them up before a diagnosis. Wait until age 78 after a parent's health crisis, and underwriting is off the table. Age 55 to 65 is the window where premiums are lower and invested savings still have time to compound.
