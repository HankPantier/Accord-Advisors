## LinkedIn

A practice paying for 15 recurring software and service charges at $60 a month is spending $10,800 a year before payroll or rent even enter the picture. Cut three unused subscriptions worth $150 a month and $1,800 comes back into cash flow, no new patients or price increase required.

Jared Hammack, CPA, has spent nearly 40 years watching optometry practices, dental offices, service businesses, and churches in Bloomington and Indianapolis lose track of exactly this kind of cost. Subscriptions are built to be forgotten: annual renewals auto-bill a year out, free trials convert without a reminder anyone reads, and software bundles pile up features nobody uses.

The fix is a simple audit, repeated monthly or quarterly, not a one-time cleanup. Read how to run one that holds up: https://accordadvisors.com/resources/take-back-control-of-subscription-creep
