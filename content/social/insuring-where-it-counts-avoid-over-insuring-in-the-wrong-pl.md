## LinkedIn

A $400,000 umbrella policy sitting next to a malpractice tail gap that could bankrupt the practice: it's one of the most common insurance mismatches we see among optometry and dental practice owners in Bloomington and Indianapolis. The fix isn't more coverage. It's coverage sized to what's actually at risk: life insurance at 10 to 15 times income, disability at 60 to 70% of income, umbrella policies matched to net worth, and business coverage tied to your practice's revenue and fixed costs. Jared Hammack, CPA, breaks down the math practice owners should run before renewing or adding any policy, including where malpractice tail coverage and buy-sell funding get overlooked. If your coverage hasn't been revisited since before a mortgage refinance or a new partner joined the practice, it's worth the read. Full breakdown here: https://accordadvisors.com/resources/insuring-where-it-counts-avoid-over-insuring-in-the-wrong-pl
