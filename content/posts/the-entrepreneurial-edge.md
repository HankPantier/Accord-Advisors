## Why local entrepreneurs need more than a bookkeeper

Running a practice or a service business in Bloomington takes more than good instincts and a shoebox of receipts. It takes someone who can translate the numbers into decisions: when to hire, when to hold off on that new equipment lease, when a tax move needs to happen before December 31st instead of after.

Accord Advisors works with business owners, practice managers, and church leaders across Bloomington and Indianapolis who want a financial advisor who shows up before tax season, not just during it. Jared Hammack, CPA, built the firm's approach around proactive guidance: monthly clarity, not year-end surprises. That's the entrepreneurial edge we bring to every client relationship, whether you're running an optometry practice on the west side or a growing service business downtown.

## Real-time insight into how your business is performing

Most business owners don't lie awake worrying about their tax return. They lie awake wondering if last month was actually profitable, whether payroll is sustainable at current volume, or why cash feels tight when the appointment book is full. That gap between activity and understanding is where a lot of financial stress lives.

Accord Advisors closes that gap with outsourced accounting delivered through a secure online platform, giving practice managers and business owners access to current financial data instead of a stack of paper that shows up three months late. You see revenue trends, expense patterns, and cash position as they develop, not after the fact.

That means fewer year-end surprises and more decisions made with actual information. If you're an optometry practice manager trying to plan for a new associate, or a service business owner deciding whether to add a second crew, you need this month's numbers, not last quarter's. Fixed monthly fees mean you can ask questions as often as you need to without watching a meter run.

## Building a strong financial foundation from day one

The decisions you make in a business's first year, entity structure, bookkeeping systems, payroll setup, follow you for a decade. Fix them later and you're often paying twice: once for the original mistake, once for the cleanup.

Business Foundation Services exist for exactly this stage. Whether you're opening a new optometry practice, launching a service business, or helping a growing church formalize its finances, Accord Advisors sets up the structure correctly the first time.

- Entity selection and setup guidance suited to your goals and liability exposure
- Bookkeeping systems built to track what actually matters for your industry
- Payroll structure that keeps you compliant from the first paycheck
- A clear starting point for financial reporting as you grow

Getting this right early means fewer amended filings, fewer awkward conversations with the IRS, and a foundation that supports growth instead of complicating it.

## Industry expertise that speaks your language

A church budget doesn't look like a dental practice's P&L, and neither should the advice behind it. Nearly 40 years serving healthcare providers has taught the firm the specific rhythms of each industry it works with, not generic small-business advice repackaged with a new logo.

### Optometry Practices
icon: Stethoscope

Practice management metrics, associate compensation structures, and equipment financing decisions specific to optometry. Many optometry clients arrive through referral partners familiar with the practice management side of the business.

### Dentists
icon: HeartPulse

Production-based compensation, equipment financing cycles, and staffing costs that shift with patient volume. Dental practices need financials that reflect chair time, not just monthly totals.

### Medical Professionals
icon: ClipboardCheck

Reimbursement timing, payer mix shifts, and the cash flow gaps they create. Medical practices need a CPA who has actually reconciled an insurance remittance before.

### Service Businesses
icon: Wrench

Job costing, equipment planning, and fleet decisions that determine whether growth adds profit or just adds overhead.

### Churches
icon: Building

Staffing compliance, restricted fund tracking, and the reporting a board or congregation expects to see. Nonprofit accounting has its own rules, and they matter.

## Fixed fees so you always know what to expect

Hourly billing creates a strange incentive: every phone call, every quick question, adds to a bill you can't predict. Business owners learn to stop calling, which means problems surface later and cost more to fix.

Accord Advisors bills flat monthly fees instead. You know the cost of outsourced accounting before the month starts, and you can call with a question about payroll, a new hire, or a tax notice without doing mental math about what it's costing you. That predictability is what turns a CPA relationship into an actual advisory relationship, one where you get CFO-level insight at a fraction of what an in-house hire would cost, without the invoice anxiety.
