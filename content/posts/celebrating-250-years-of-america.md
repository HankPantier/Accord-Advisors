## Honoring a milestone for our community and clients

In 2026, the United States turns 250 years old, a milestone that touches every town, practice, and small business built since 1776. Bloomington has its own chapter in that story, from Kirkwood Avenue storefronts to the optometry practices and medical clinics that keep this community healthy. RootAdvisors has spent nearly 40 years working alongside the people who write that local chapter every day: practice owners, service business operators, and church administrators across Bloomington and Indianapolis. This anniversary is a moment to recognize their work. Two hundred fifty years of American enterprise did not happen in boardrooms alone. It happened in small offices, exam rooms, and Sunday services, one decision at a time. That is the story worth marking here.

## Small businesses as the backbone of the American story

America's economic history is mostly a small business history. Optometrists opening their first practice, dentists taking over a retiring partner's patient base, service businesses filling a gap nobody else would touch: these are the entrepreneurial moves that built the country's tax base, job market, and Main Streets long before national chains existed. Bloomington and Indianapolis carry that tradition forward today. The optometry practices RootAdvisors serves, many referred through Acquios, represent the same independent spirit that shaped commerce two and a half centuries ago. Medical professionals opening solo practices, dentists managing patient growth, and service business owners scaling a second location each take on real financial risk to build something that outlasts them. That entrepreneurial persistence, more than any single invention or policy, is what 250 years of American business actually looks like on the ground in central Indiana.

## Supporting the practices and organizations that serve our community

Healthcare practices, service businesses, and churches do more than generate revenue. They keep people healthy, keep homes and equipment running, and keep congregations connected. Bloomington and Indianapolis depend on all three showing up strong, month after month. RootAdvisors' outsourced accounting and Business Foundation Services exist to help those organizations stay standing long after the ribbon-cutting.

- Entity setup and bookkeeping systems for churches and startups getting their financial foundation right the first time
- Fixed monthly fees instead of hourly billing, so a phone call to ask a question never turns into a surprise invoice
- A secure online platform for exchanging documents and reviewing financials without a filing cabinet or a fax machine
- Nearly 40 years of healthcare-specific accounting experience, so optometrists and dentists get answers grounded in the realities of their industry

## A look back at nearly 40 years serving Bloomington and Indianapolis

RootAdvisors' own history is a small, local version of the same story being marked nationally in 2026. Jared Hammack, CPA, has built the firm's practice around one idea: healthcare providers and local business owners deserve accounting support that goes beyond tax season. Almost four decades of serving optometry practices, dental offices, and service businesses in Bloomington and Indianapolis has meant adapting through recessions, tax law changes, and the shift from paper ledgers to secure online platforms. What has not changed is the commitment to answering the phone, explaining the numbers in plain language, and treating every client relationship as a long-term one. Continuity is not an accident. It takes the same kind of steady, generation-spanning work that built the country this anniversary celebrates.

## Ways Bloomington is celebrating America's 250th anniversary

Communities across Indiana are marking 2026 with local programming, and Bloomington is no exception. Expect community festivals, historical exhibits, and civic events throughout the year honoring the anniversary, alongside programming from Indiana University Bloomington and local historical societies. Monroe County businesses, churches, and civic groups are expected to take part with their own commemorations, from special services to Main Street gatherings. For the practices and organizations RootAdvisors serves, these events are also a reminder of something practical: the businesses participating, optometry practices, dental offices, churches, and service businesses, are the same ones that need a strong financial foundation to keep showing up for the next 250 years.
