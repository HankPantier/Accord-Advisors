## Why tax season feels heavier for local business owners and practices

Spring in Bloomington and Indianapolis means blooming trees, March Madness watch parties, and for optometrists, dentists, physicians, service business owners, and church administrators, a familiar tightening in the chest. Tax season doesn't arrive in isolation. It lands on top of patient scheduling, payroll runs, vendor payments, and the daily work of keeping a practice or organization moving.

For a solo optometry practice managing four exam lanes, or a church treasurer juggling volunteer payroll and building fund reporting, the paperwork compounds fast. Receipts pile up. Prior-year numbers get fuzzy. Estimated payments come due while W-2s and 1099s still need sorting.

None of this means you're behind schedule. It means tax season asks more of business owners than most calendars allow for, especially when the books haven't been kept current all year. That's a solvable problem, not a personal failing.

## What makes this tax season different

Rule changes surface every year, and this one brought its share: adjusted deduction thresholds, updated depreciation limits, and reporting requirements that shifted for both S-corps and sole proprietors. Local accountants across Bloomington have fielded more questions than usual this season, from new complications in standard filings to clients caught off guard by provisions they assumed would carry over unchanged.

None of this has to cause a scramble if someone is watching the changes as they happen. Jared Hammack, CPA, tracks these updates as part of the ongoing outsourced accounting relationship Accord Advisors keeps with clients, not as a once-a-year review. When a rule shifts, whether it affects an optometry practice's equipment depreciation or a service business's estimated payments, the adjustment gets made in your books months before your return is due, not the week it's filed.

## A steady month-by-month path instead of a scramble

Outsourced accounting through Accord Advisors runs on a fixed monthly fee, not hourly billing, so the work happens all year instead of piling up in March.

### Books closed every month
Your transactions get categorized and reconciled monthly, so there's no January scramble to reconstruct twelve months of activity from a shoebox of receipts.

### Quarterly check-ins, not April surprises
Estimated payments and cash flow get reviewed each quarter, catching a tax liability problem in July instead of discovering it in April.

### A pre-filing review in January
Before any return gets prepared, your Accord Advisors team walks through the prior year's numbers with you and flags anything that needs attention early.

### Filing day becomes a formality
Because the books are already clean and the estimates already made, your return gets prepared from data that's been accurate since the first week of the year.

## Getting your business foundation right before you file

A messy tax season often traces back to a foundation problem from a year or two earlier: an entity structure that no longer fits the business, recordkeeping that never got set up properly, or compliance basics that got skipped during a busy launch.

Business Foundation Services address this directly. For a startup optometry practice or a growing service business, that means choosing the right entity type, setting up a chart of accounts that actually reflects the business, and putting basic compliance habits in place from day one. Get this right early, and tax season stops being a fire drill. It becomes the point where a year of clean, organized data simply gets filed.

## A secure, simple way to work with your accountant this season

Gathering documents during a busy month shouldn't mean scanning files at midnight or emailing sensitive numbers back and forth. Accord Advisors clients use a secure online platform built for exactly this:

- Upload W-2s, 1099s, and receipts from a phone or laptop, any time
- Message your accounting team directly instead of playing phone tag
- Check the status of your return without waiting for a callback
- Sign and submit documents electronically once your return is ready

One login. One place for everything. No stack of paper on the passenger seat on the way to a downtown Bloomington office.
