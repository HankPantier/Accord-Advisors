## Making a change in your financial advisor is simpler than you think

Switching accountants feels like a bigger decision than it actually is. Most business owners in Bloomington put off the conversation for years, worried about disrupting payroll, losing historical records, or starting from scratch with someone new. None of that has to happen.

RootAdvisors handles the move from your current bookkeeper or CPA with a clear plan and a fixed monthly fee, so you know exactly what to expect from day one. There's no upheaval to daily operations, no gap in your books, and no surprise invoice waiting at the end. Jared Hammack, CPA, walks new clients through the switch personally, reviewing existing records and setting a start date that works around your schedule.

If you're reading this page, something about your current setup isn't working the way it should. That's reason enough to have a conversation.

## Signs it is time to upgrade your accounting support

You don't need a dramatic falling-out with your current accountant to know it's time for a change. Bloomington business owners describe the same handful of frustrations:

- No visibility into how the practice performed last month until tax time rolls around
- Surprise tax bills that show up in March with no warning
- Hourly billing that makes every phone call feel like it costs money
- A bookkeeper who handles data entry but can't explain what the numbers mean
- A CPA who treats a dental practice, a church, and a retail shop the same way

Any one of these is worth a second look. Two or three together usually mean you've outgrown the relationship you're in, not that you did anything wrong. Businesses and practices grow past their first accountant all the time. The mismatch shows up in the same places: monthly reporting, tax planning, and industry-specific advice that never quite arrives.

## Built for optometrists, dentists, medical professionals, service businesses, and churches

RootAdvisors isn't a generalist firm that added healthcare and church clients to a stack of retail and manufacturing accounts. The niches below are the practice.

### Optometry Practices
icon: Stethoscope

Nearly 40 years working with healthcare providers means the firm already knows what matters to an optometry practice manager: patient volume, insurance reimbursement timing, and equipment financing. Many optometrists arrive through referrals in their own professional networks.

### Dentists
icon: HeartPulse

Dental practices carry the same reimbursement complexity as medical offices, plus equipment costs that don't fit a standard depreciation schedule. Reporting gets built around the numbers that actually run a dental practice.

### Medical Professionals
icon: ClipboardCheck

Reimbursement timelines, staffing costs, and compliance requirements shape a medical practice's books differently than a typical small business. Monthly reporting reflects that reality from the start.

### Service Businesses
icon: Wrench

Contractors, consultants, and other service-based businesses need job costing and cash flow visibility a generic bookkeeper often misses. Reports get built around the work you actually bill.

### Churches
icon: Building2

Religious organizations answer to a congregation, a board, and sometimes a denomination, each with different reporting expectations. Compliance and financial reporting get handled so staff can focus on ministry.

## How the transition works

Switching firms sounds complicated until you see the actual steps.

### A conversation, not a sales pitch
A straightforward call about where your business or practice stands today: current accountant, reporting gaps, and what you actually need.

### A review of your current setup
Jared Hammack, CPA, reviews existing books, tax filings, and software before quoting a fixed monthly fee, so the number reflects the real scope of the work.

### A clean transition of your records
RootAdvisors coordinates directly with your outgoing bookkeeper or CPA to move records onto the secure online platform, so nothing gets lost and nothing gets duplicated.

### Your first month on the platform
You get logged into your dashboard, see your first monthly reporting package, and have a direct line to ask questions, no hourly clock running in the background.
