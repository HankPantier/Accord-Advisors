## Bite sized guidance for busy practice owners and business managers

Running an optometry practice, a dental office, or a service business leaves little time to research every financial question that pops up between patients or projects. QuickReads is a library of short, plain-language articles built for exactly that gap. Each one takes a few minutes to read, skips the jargon, and gives you a direct answer you can act on before your next staff meeting.

Jared Hammack, CPA, and the Accord Advisors team pull these topics from actual client conversations in Bloomington and Indianapolis, not a generic content calendar. If you sign the checks, manage the books, or make the call on a new hire, this is where you get clarity without booking a meeting first.

## Written for optometrists, dentists, and medical practices

A practice manager reviewing insurance reimbursements has a different question than a general business owner asking about payroll. Nearly 40 years advising optometrists, dentists, and medical professionals means Accord Advisors already knows what those questions usually are.

Jared Hammack, CPA, leads much of this content, drawing on client work across [optometry practices](/industries/optometry), [dentists](/industries/dentists), and [medical professionals](/industries/medical-professionals). Topics range from reading your [practice management metrics](/industries/optometry/practice-management-metrics) to handling [reimbursement challenges](/industries/medical-professionals/reimbursement-challenges) without losing a week to paperwork.

## Insights for service businesses and churches

Healthcare isn't the only niche with its own rulebook. Service businesses juggle job costing, equipment financing, and fleet decisions that a generic accounting article never quite addresses. Churches and religious organizations face fund accounting, staffing rules, and fraud risks that most CPA firms rarely discuss in plain language.

QuickReads covers both directly, from [fleet and equipment planning](/industries/service-businesses/fleet-and-equipment-planning) for service businesses to [staffing and compliance](/industries/churches/staffing-and-compliance) for churches, plus practical reads like [fraud prevention strategies for nonprofit organizations](/fraud-prevention-strategies-for-nonprofit-organizations).
