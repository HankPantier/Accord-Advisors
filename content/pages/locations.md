## Contact Accord Advisors

Whether you run an optometry practice in Bloomington, manage a service business near Indianapolis, or need personal tax help before the next deadline, a real person picks up when you call. Jared Hammack, CPA, leads a team that has spent nearly 40 years working with healthcare providers, churches, and growing businesses across central Indiana. No phone trees, no runaround, just a direct line to people who already understand your industry.

## Bloomington Office

The Bloomington office sits at 1516 South Walnut Street, Bloomington, IN 47401, a short drive from downtown and easy to find along the South Walnut corridor. Call 812-332-7200 to schedule a visit or ask a quick question about a tax notice, payroll issue, or bookkeeping backlog. This is the firm's original home office, where Jared Hammack, CPA, and the outsourced accounting team handle work for optometry practices, medical professionals, and service businesses throughout Monroe County. Parking is available on-site, and appointments are recommended so the right specialist, whether that's a bookkeeper, payroll contact, or tax preparer, is ready when you arrive. Ask your map app for directions to South Walnut Street in Bloomington, or call ahead and staff will walk you through it.

## Avon Office

For clients closer to Indianapolis, the Avon office is located at 7386 Business Center Drive, Suite B, Avon, IN 46123, just west of the Indianapolis metro. Call 317-387-9881 to reach the Avon team directly. This location serves many of the optometry, dental, and medical practice clients referred through Acquios, along with churches and service businesses across Hendricks County and the broader Indianapolis area. Suite B sits on the ground floor with parking directly out front, so walking in for a scheduled appointment is straightforward. Because both offices share the same secure online platform and fixed monthly fee structure, moving between Bloomington and Avon for a meeting never means starting over with new paperwork or a new point of contact.
