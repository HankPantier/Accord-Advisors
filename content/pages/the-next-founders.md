## What founders need most in the early stages

Most founders don't need a lecture on accounting principles. They need to know, month to month, whether the business is actually working. In practice, that means:

- Knowing monthly revenue, expenses, and margin without digging through spreadsheets
- Financial and operational dashboards that show what's changing and why
- A clear separation between personal and business finances from day one
- Someone to flag a cash flow problem before it becomes a crisis
- Tax and entity structure decisions made early, not fixed later at a penalty

Without this, founders end up making decisions on instinct instead of data. Outsourced accounting and foundational setup work exist to close exactly this gap, and they're most valuable in the first 12 to 18 months of a business, not after year three when the mess is bigger.
