## An outsourced accounting department built around your business

Between patient schedules, staff turnover, and the daily demands of running a practice or business, most owners don't have time to reconcile accounts or track down last month's numbers. That's the job Accord Advisors takes off your plate.

We work as your [outsourced accounting department](/services/outsourced-accounting): bookkeeping, financial statements, and tax planning handled by one team instead of scattered across software subscriptions and part-time hires. Every engagement runs on a fixed monthly fee, not an hourly clock that punishes you for asking a question.

Clients across Bloomington and Indianapolis log into a secure online platform to see real numbers whenever they need them, not only at tax time. Jared Hammack, CPA, leads the firm with a direct approach: know the business, catch problems early, and give owners a clear read on where things stand every month.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: optometry-dental-practice-review.jpg | alt: "Practice manager and accountant reviewing optometry practice financial reports" | query: "optometry dental office financial review" -->
## Support for optometry and dental practices

Optometry and dental practices run on thin margins between insurance reimbursements, equipment costs, and staff pay, and generic bookkeeping misses what matters most. Accord Advisors has worked inside healthcare numbers for nearly 40 years, long enough to know how a healthy [optometry practice's](/industries/optometry) books should look compared to one that's quietly losing money.

Many optometrist clients arrive through Acquios, the network practice managers rely on for vetted back-office partners. Once onboard, practices get monthly financials that reflect how the business actually performed: revenue by provider, product line profitability, and expense trends against the prior month. [Dentists](/industries/dentists) get the same attention, with a focus on equipment financing and staffing costs specific to a dental office.

Instead of piecing together spreadsheets between patients, practice managers get a dashboard and a CPA who already understands optometry and dental economics.

## Guidance for medical professionals and healthcare providers

Physicians and other healthcare providers deal with reimbursement cycles, coding changes, and compliance requirements that a general-practice CPA firm often treats as an afterthought. Accord Advisors builds bookkeeping and tax strategy around how [medical professionals](/industries/medical-professionals) actually get paid.

- Monthly financials that separate clinical revenue from ancillary income
- Tax planning that accounts for entity structure, retirement contributions, and reimbursement timing
- Direct answers from a CPA who has worked healthcare books for years, not a call center
- Support during payer audits or reimbursement disputes without starting from scratch

## Accounting for service-based businesses

Service businesses, consultants, contractors, agencies, wellness providers, share a common frustration: knowing how last month went before it's too late to act on it. Accord Advisors builds financial dashboards around the metrics that matter for each [service business](/industries/service-businesses), not a generic template pulled from off-the-shelf software.

Whether a business is two years old and still finding its footing, or twenty years in and planning an ownership transition, the numbers need to answer the same question: how did we actually do, and what should change next month? Fixed monthly fees mean owners can call with a quick question without watching the clock, and the secure online platform keeps records available whenever they're needed, not just when the books close.

## Financial stewardship for churches and religious organizations

Congregations, boards, and donors expect clear answers about where contributions go, and compliance requirements around payroll, housing allowances, and nonprofit status don't leave much room for guesswork.

Accord Advisors handles the bookkeeping and reporting that keeps [church](/industries/churches) leadership confident in front of a board or a congregation: fund accounting, payroll for pastoral staff, and financial statements that make stewardship visible instead of assumed. Fixed monthly fees keep costs predictable for organizations that run on tight, donor-funded budgets. Churches get a CPA who understands the difference between a for-profit ledger and a stewardship report, and who treats every dollar with the transparency a congregation deserves.

## Business foundation services to get you started right

Starting a business means dozens of decisions before the first invoice goes out: entity structure, bank accounts, bookkeeping systems, payroll setup. Get these wrong early and they're expensive to unwind later. [Business Foundation Services](/services/business-foundation-services) set new and growing businesses up with the structure that lets everything after run smoothly.

- Entity selection and setup guidance, LLC, S-corp, or otherwise
- Chart of accounts and bookkeeping systems built for how the business actually operates
- Payroll and vendor systems ready before day one
- A foundation built to scale as the business grows, not one that needs rebuilding at year three

## Personal income tax planning alongside your business needs

Business owners rarely have simple personal returns. K-1s, quarterly estimates, retirement contributions, and the tax impact of business decisions all flow onto the individual return, and separating that work from the business relationship creates blind spots.

Accord Advisors handles [personal income tax](/services/personal-income-tax) preparation and planning for owners, practice shareholders, and individuals who just want their return done right. Because the same team sees both sides, a decision made in the business, a distribution, an equipment purchase, a change in entity, gets planned for on the personal return before it becomes a surprise in April.

**Q: How does the fixed monthly fee work instead of hourly billing?**
A: Clients pay one predictable monthly fee covering bookkeeping, financial reporting, and advisory support, rather than being billed hourly for calls or questions. This lets business owners and practice managers reach out for guidance without worrying about the meter running.

**Q: Can Accord Advisors handle my personal taxes along with my business accounting?**
A: Yes. Because the same team manages both, business decisions, distributions, entity changes, or equipment purchases, get planned for on the personal return in advance, rather than showing up as a surprise the following April.
