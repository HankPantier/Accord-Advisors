## Outsourced Accounting Built for Law Firms

Running a law practice means billing clients, managing casework, and staying on top of court deadlines. Bookkeeping tends to slide to the bottom of the list until trust account errors or missed payroll deadlines force the issue. Accord Advisors steps in as your outsourced accounting department, handling the financial side of your practice so you and your partners can stay focused on cases.

Solo attorneys and small firms rarely need a full-time controller, but they do need accurate books, timely reporting, and someone who understands the specific compliance pressures law firms face. The Accord Advisors team manages your books, payroll, and trust account reconciliations through a secure online platform, replacing the guesswork of hiring and training in-house staff with a fixed, predictable monthly fee.

## Trust Account Oversight & Compliance

IOLTA and client trust accounts carry rules that don't bend. Commingling client funds with operating funds, even by accident, can trigger a bar complaint or worse. Accord Advisors reconciles trust accounts on a set schedule so every dollar is accounted for and traceable back to the client matter it belongs to.

The firm's process includes three-way reconciliations that match the trust ledger, the bank statement, and individual client balances against each other. Discrepancies get flagged and resolved before they become a compliance problem, not after an audit request lands on your desk.

Attorneys don't need to become bookkeepers to stay compliant with bar association trust accounting rules. They need a system that catches errors early and documentation that holds up if a regulator asks questions. That's the standard applied to every trust account under management, whether the firm has one attorney or ten.

## Partner Distributions & Payroll Management

Partner compensation in a law firm rarely follows a simple formula. Origination credits, seniority tiers, and profit-sharing arrangements all factor into what each partner takes home, and the math needs to be right every time.

- Partner distribution calculations based on your firm's compensation formula, whether that's lockstep, eat-what-you-kill, or a modified hybrid
- Scheduled distribution processing so partners are paid consistently and on time
- Payroll processing for associates, paralegals, and administrative staff, including tax withholding and filings
- Clear records separating partner draws from firm operating expenses for tax and compliance purposes
- Coordination with your firm's tax preparer to keep K-1s and distribution records aligned at year-end

Getting distributions wrong creates friction between partners and headaches at tax time. Accurate, timely processing keeps the numbers clean and the partnership running smoothly.

## CFO-Level Financial Guidance

Most law firms don't have the budget for a full-time CFO, and most don't need one. What they need is someone who can look at the numbers and tell them what's actually happening in the practice.

Cash flow forecasting matters more in a law firm than most businesses, given the gap between billable hours worked and collections received. Profitability by practice area shows which parts of the firm generate margin and which are quietly draining resources. Fractional CFO guidance from Accord Advisors covers both, along with strategic planning around growth, associate hiring, and succession timing for partners approaching retirement.

This level of insight typically costs a firm six figures a year in salary for an in-house hire. Clients get it as part of a fixed monthly engagement instead.
