## Send documents securely

Tax returns, payroll records, and financial statements contain information you don't want floating through unsecured email. Our secure file transfer option encrypts documents from the moment you upload them until our team opens them, so sensitive data stays protected at every step.

Use this option whenever you need to send:

- W-2s, 1099s, and other tax forms
- Bank and credit card statements
- Payroll records or employee data
- Signed engagement letters or authorization forms
- Practice or business financial reports

If you're unsure which method to use for a specific document, contact our team through the [forms and documents](/forms-documents-links) page and we'll point you in the right direction.
