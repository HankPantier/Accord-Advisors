## Meet the author behind our insights

Jared Hammack, CPA, writes the articles you'll find in this section of the RootAdvisors site. He's spent his career inside the numbers of optometry practices, medical offices, service businesses, and churches across Bloomington and Indianapolis, work that traces back to a firm history built on nearly 40 years of serving healthcare providers.

Jared isn't a marketing writer assigned to cover accounting topics. He's a CPA who reviews financial statements, answers client calls about payroll questions, and sits across the table during tax planning sessions. What he publishes here comes from those conversations: the questions practice owners actually ask, the mistakes he's helped clients avoid, and the fixes that worked. When he writes about cash flow or reimbursement delays, it's because he's walked a client through that exact problem the same month.

## Areas of expertise

### Optometry Practices
icon: Target

Jared works closely with optometry practice managers and shareholders, many referred through Acquios, on the metrics that keep a practice financially healthy: staff productivity, insurance reimbursement timing, and monthly performance benchmarks.

### Dental Practices
icon: HeartPulse

Dental owners face many of the same overhead and staffing pressures as optometrists. Jared applies the same outsourced accounting discipline to keep dental practices current on their numbers.

### Medical Professionals
icon: Stethoscope

From solo physicians to multi-provider clinics, Jared writes about the reimbursement complexity and compliance issues that make healthcare accounting different from a standard small business ledger.

### Service Businesses
icon: Briefcase

Contractors, consultants, and other service-based owners need a clear read on monthly performance. Jared covers the financial and operational data these businesses rely on to make decisions.

### Churches
icon: Building2

Religious organizations answer to boards, donors, and IRS rules all at once. Jared's articles on church accounting address staffing compliance and financial oversight specific to nonprofits.

## Why our team writes about outsourced accounting

Most accounting content online is generic. RootAdvisors publishes articles from advisors who actually do the work, because business owners searching for answers deserve something more specific than a recycled blog post.

Jared and the rest of the team write to answer the questions clients bring to the office: how to read a monthly financial statement, what a business valuation actually involves, or how a fixed monthly fee compares to hourly billing. The goal isn't to publish frequently for its own sake. It's to give practice managers, church treasurers, and business owners a plain-language resource they can trust before they ever pick up the phone.
