## A trusted advisor, not just a tax preparer

Most CPA firms show up once a year, hand you a tax return, and disappear until next April. Accord Advisors works differently. Jared Hammack, CPA, and the team function as an outsourced accounting department, staying involved in your books, your cash flow, and your decisions every month of the year.

That kind of ongoing relationship means fewer surprises at tax time and faster answers when you need them. Instead of walking someone new through a full year of transactions, you get a CPA who already understands your practice, your industry, and the goals you're working toward.

For business owners in Bloomington and across Indianapolis, that consistency matters. You're not hiring a firm to file paperwork. You're bringing on an advisor who treats your numbers as a living part of running the business, not a once-a-year chore.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: fixed-fee-invoice-review.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing a monthly invoice statement with calculator nearby" | query: "small business owner reviewing invoice" -->
## Fixed monthly fees instead of surprise bills

Hourly billing turns every phone call and every question into a line item you have to think twice about. Accord Advisors charges a fixed monthly fee instead, so you know exactly what accounting and advisory support costs before the month even starts.

That predictability changes how business owners use their CPA. Instead of rationing questions to avoid a bigger invoice, clients call when something comes up, whether it's a vendor contract, a hiring decision, or a tax question that can't wait until April. The fee stays the same either way.

Budgeting for professional services becomes simple: one number, billed monthly, with no meter running in the background. For practice managers and owners juggling payroll, vendors, and their own paychecks, that predictability removes one more variable to worry about.

## Clear monthly insight into how your practice or business is doing

Business owners don't need a stack of spreadsheets. They need to know, in plain language, how last month went and what it means for the next one.

- Monthly financial statements delivered on a schedule you can count on
- Dashboards that translate raw numbers into decisions you can act on
- Non-financial metrics tracked alongside the financials, from patient volume to job completion rates
- Direct access to a CPA who can explain what the numbers mean, not just report them

## A secure online platform built for busy owners

Practice managers and business owners don't have time to chase down paperwork or wait on hold. Accord Advisors' secure online platform puts document sharing, financial reports, and communication in one place, accessible whenever you need it.

Upload a bill, review last month's financials, or send a question to your CPA without leaving your desk or waiting for office hours. Everything runs through a secure connection built to protect sensitive financial data, which matters as much to a healthcare practice as it does to a service business or church office.

For clients splitting time between running a practice and running a household, that convenience adds up. Less time on logistics means more time on the parts of the business only you can handle.

## Local roots serving Bloomington, Indianapolis, and Avon

Accord Advisors is based in Bloomington, Indiana, with clients across Indianapolis and Avon. That local footprint means Jared Hammack, CPA, and the team understand Indiana tax rules, local business conditions, and the kind of practices and organizations that make up this part of the state.

National firms and out-of-state advisors can file a return from anywhere. They can't sit across the table from you, learn your staff by name, or understand why a slow winter hits an Indianapolis optometry practice differently than it hits a service business in Avon.

Working with a Bloomington-based CPA means working with people who are part of the same community you are, not a call center three time zones away.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Why business owners choose Accord Advisors

**Q: What makes Accord Advisors different from other CPA firms in Bloomington?**

**Q: How does the fixed monthly fee model work?**
A: Instead of hourly billing, Accord Advisors sets one predictable monthly fee for outsourced accounting and advisory services. Clients know the cost upfront, can call with questions without worrying about extra charges, and can budget for professional support the same way they budget for payroll or rent.

**Q: What kind of reporting do clients receive each month?**
A: Clients get monthly financial statements and dashboards that translate raw numbers into plain-language insight, plus relevant non-financial metrics like patient volume or job completion rates. The goal is a clear picture of performance without requiring an accounting background to interpret it.
