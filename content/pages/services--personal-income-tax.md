## Personal tax help that goes beyond filing a return

For most people, taxes are a once-a-year scramble: gather the documents, drop them off somewhere, sign what you're handed, and hope for the best. That works fine if your financial life is simple. It works less well if you own a practice, run a service business, or make decisions today that carry tax consequences three years down the road.

Accord Advisors treats personal income tax preparation as a year-round relationship, not a spring transaction. Clients in Bloomington and Indianapolis bring us questions in July about a home sale, in October about a retirement account, in January about a bonus. We're already thinking about next year's return while we finish this year's. That's a different posture than a seasonal storefront preparer can offer, and it's the one we take with every individual client on our books.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: personal-tax-planning-meeting.jpg | alt: "Advisor and client reviewing personal tax documents at a desk" | query: "financial advisor client meeting documents" -->
## Who we help with personal tax planning

Most of our individual tax clients aren't purely individuals. They're business owners, optometry practice shareholders, physicians, dentists, and service business operators who also need someone thinking about their household return. A lot of firms split that work: one preparer for the business, another for the personal return, neither talking to the other.

We don't split it. The same advisor who handles your [practice's outsourced accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting) or [business foundation services](/services/business-foundation-services) also prepares your personal return, because your K-1, your distributions, and your retirement contributions all flow through one financial picture. Optometrists and [medical professionals](/industries/medical-professionals) referred to us through their industry networks tell us this coordination is the piece they were missing before.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: tax-planning-checklist-review.jpg | alt: "CPA pointing to a tax planning checklist with a client" | query: "accountant explaining tax documents client" -->
## What our personal tax preparation and planning includes

A personal return prepared once a year, without context, is a guess dressed up as accuracy. Ours starts earlier and covers more ground:

- Accurate, complete preparation of federal and Indiana state returns, reviewed by a CPA before filing
- Proactive tax planning throughout the year, not a single meeting in March
- Guidance around major life events: retirement distributions, sale of a practice, [succession planning](/services/succession-planning), or a business sale that changes your tax picture
- Estimated payment calculations so you're never guessing what to send the IRS quarterly
- Coordination with your business accounting so personal and business tax strategy don't work against each other
- Plain-language explanations of what changed on your return and why, before you ever sign it

The goal is simple: no surprises in April, and no scrambling to reconstruct a year's worth of decisions in the two weeks before the deadline.

## Fixed fees and a secure client experience

Hourly billing turns every phone call and every question into a math problem. Wondering whether it's worth calling your accountant shouldn't be part of tax planning. That's why Accord Advisors prices personal tax preparation and planning as a fixed monthly or annual fee, agreed to upfront, so you know the cost before the work starts and can call as often as you need without a meter running.

Documents move through a secure online client portal instead of email attachments or a folder of paper dropped off at a front desk. Upload a W-2, a 1099, or a closing statement whenever it arrives, and it's there for us to work with immediately. No physical files sitting in a car, no faxing, no waiting for an office to reopen.

## Why business owners choose Accord Advisors for personal tax

Owners who run a practice or a service business have a personal return that's rarely simple. Distributions, retirement plan contributions, entity structure, and depreciation choices on the business side all land on the personal return eventually. Handling those separately from two different firms invites gaps.

Our CPA leads tax planning for individuals alongside the firm's outsourced accounting and business foundation clients, which means the same advisor who sees your monthly financials also prepares your 1040. That's CFO-level insight applied to a personal return, not just data entry at tax time. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Personal income tax preparation and planning

**Q: Does Accord Advisors prepare personal tax returns for business owners?**
A: Yes. Most individual clients also own a practice or service business, so personal returns are coordinated with business accounting by the same advisor. This keeps K-1s, distributions, and retirement contributions aligned across both returns instead of handled separately.

**Q: How much does personal income tax preparation cost?**
A: Accord Advisors uses fixed monthly or annual fees agreed to upfront, not hourly billing. You'll know the cost before work begins and can ask questions throughout the year without worrying about a running meter.

**Q: Do you serve clients outside Bloomington?**
A: Yes, Indianapolis is a core service area alongside Bloomington. Documents move through a secure online portal, so location rarely limits how closely we work together on tax planning.

**Q: What if my tax situation involves selling a business or retiring?**
A: These are exactly the moments personal tax planning matters most. We guide clients through practice sales, succession transitions, and retirement distributions well before filing, so the tax impact is planned for rather than discovered.
