# About Accord Advisors

## Identity

Accord Advisors (rootadvisors.com) is a CPA firm with offices in Bloomington, Indiana (1516 South Walnut Street) and Avon, Indiana (7386 Business Center Drive, Suite B), serving the greater Indianapolis area and clients nationwide. The firm can be reached at 812-332-7200 or office@accordadvisors.com.

The firm's roots trace to Ally Accounting, founded in 2010 by Managing Partner Jared Hammack, a CPA since 2006 and Indiana University graduate who previously worked in international/complex tax at Deloitte. Ally Accounting grew through the acquisition of a local Indianapolis firm. Ryan Deckard also serves as Managing Partner. The team includes client managers, client accounting specialists, bookkeeping and payroll specialists, and administrative staff supporting day-to-day client service, including Kelly Root (Firm Administrator), Sean Hanthorn (Director of Operations), Jill Bailey, Amy Young, and Anita Altop (Client Managers), Wendi Elmore (Manager), Rachel Logston (Payroll Specialist), and others.

The firm is affiliated with the AICPA and the Indiana CPA Society. Its tone is approachable, trustworthy, reassuring, professional, supportive, and straightforward — blending accounting expertise with plain-language communication aimed at giving clients relief from financial burdens.

## Positioning & Differentiation

Accord Advisors positions itself as an outsourced accounting department and trusted advisor for business owners, medical and optometry practices, service businesses, churches, and individuals — supporting clients from startup through growth and succession. Rather than billing hourly, the firm charges fixed monthly fees and delivers services through a secure online platform.

Key differentiators include:
- Acting as an outsourced accounting department, providing CFO-level business management insight at a fraction of the cost of an in-house hire
- Fixed monthly fee pricing instead of hourly billing
- Deep industry-specific expertise, including nearly 40 years serving healthcare providers
- A secure online client platform
- Nationwide client reach from Indiana-based offices

Clients want to know how their business is performing each month and need relevant financial and non-financial data and dashboards to make informed decisions — a need the firm addresses through its outsourced accounting and reporting services. Optometry clients are frequently referred through Acquios (acquios.com).

## Industries Served

### Optometry Practices
Accord Advisors provides specialized accounting support for optometry practices and their retail optics operations. Pain point: managing growth and profitability across combined clinical and retail optics operations. Value proposition: industry-specific accounting support tailored to the dual clinical/retail nature of optometry practices.

### Medical Professionals
With nearly 40 years of experience, the firm provides tax, accounting, and payroll services for healthcare providers. Pain points: cost management and reimbursement challenges. Value proposition: long-tenured, healthcare-specific expertise across tax, accounting, and payroll.

### Dental Practices
The firm offers tax, accounting, and payroll services for dental practices and providers. Pain points: cost management and reimbursement challenges, similar to other healthcare providers. Value proposition: dedicated tax, accounting, and payroll support built around the financial realities of dental practices.

### Service Businesses
Accord Advisors provides back-office financial support and strategic planning for attorneys, law firms, and consulting companies. Value proposition: outsourced back-office accounting and strategic planning support for professional service firms.

### Churches
The firm provides fund accounting and financial stewardship support for churches. Pain points: staffing and compliance challenges. Value proposition: specialized fund accounting expertise to help churches maintain financial stewardship and meet compliance requirements despite staffing constraints.
